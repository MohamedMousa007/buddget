4Review · conflicts and gaps found, for your confirmation
1
2d Split sheet blocks totals over 100%
3c now says shares can overlap and fill by priority. The two conflict. 4b replaces the error state with a priority order and a calm note.
2
Paused tab had no screen
The new All / Active / Paused switch needs one. 4a shows Paused, with a line explaining it is not counted as behind. All would list Active first, then Paused.
3
Superseded screens
2b (3-step add goal), 2c (separate Buddgy chat), 2e (old goal detail) are replaced by 3b and 3d. Archive them once you confirm.
4
Push copy for months behind
Current copy says "behind schedule" with one fix. Proposed: "Vacation has been behind for 3 months. Move the date to Jun 2028 or add EGP 4,100 a month." Send once a month after the second month, not daily.
5
Quarterly goals
Cards, detail and pushes say "a month". For quarterly goals they should read "a quarter" and the status line should show the next deposit date.
6
Achieved view title
Add the same title row as the other views: "Achieved goals 3", with the trophy chip selected.
7
Cancelled goals
The old "Paused and cancelled" row is gone. Proposal: deleting a goal asks where its pocket money goes (Free pocket by default) and removes it. No cancelled list.
8
Pocket timing
Create the pocket when the goal is created, with a 0 balance, so shares and progress work from day one.
