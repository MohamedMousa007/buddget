# Design Brief — Buddget logo

Paste into Claude Design with the **Buddget design system** attached
(project `3e84a07a-da79-472d-b487-07c93afa5f48`, teal identity, re-synced 2026-09-28).

---

## What Buddget is

A personal-finance app for tracking spending, income, budgets, debts and savings. It ships as a web
PWA and as native iOS and Android apps. Its real differentiator is **automation**: it reads bank SMS
messages, matches salary deposits to expected paydays, detects subscriptions, and scans receipts —
so the user does almost no manual entry.

Primary market is the Arab world (Egypt, Saudi, UAE, and the Gulf). The app is fully bilingual
English/Arabic. Tagline: *"Your money, finally makes sense."*

**Tone:** direct, friendly, no finance jargon. It treats the user as capable — gives the number,
gives context, gets out of the way. Calm and reassuring, not hype, not gamified.

---

## What we need

1. **A logo mark (icon)** — the thing that becomes the app icon and sits at 26px in the app header.
2. **The wordmark lockup** — mark + "Buddget", horizontal.
3. Both in **light and dark** variants.

**The wordmark itself already exists and must be kept**: the word "Buddget", with the **second "d"
accented in the brand teal** — `Bud` + teal `d` + `get`. Do not redesign the word or the accent
idea; recolour and set it, and design the mark that goes beside it. Typeface is DM Sans Bold, which
is the app's heading face.

---

## Show options first

Deliver **3–4 genuinely distinct directions** before converging. This is identity work; one option
is not a choice. For each direction give a one-line rationale — what idea it encodes — so the
decision is about meaning, not just taste.

Then, for the chosen direction, deliver the full set in §5.

---

## Brand constraints

Colour comes from the design system. Do not invent new brand colours.

| | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| Brand teal (identity) | `#12B0A0` | `#1FC3B2` |
| Teal deep | `#0D8578` / `#0B7568` | — |
| On-teal | `#FFFFFF` | `#04201C` |

The mark may use a teal gradient (the app's hero uses `#0E9384 → #0A534A`). It must also work as a
**single flat colour** — that is the test of whether the shape is doing the work.

---

## Hard rules

- 🚫 **No pig, piggy bank, or any pig reference, in any form, ever.** This is a religious constraint
  for the app's audience, not a style preference. It is non-negotiable and applies to every asset.
- 🚫 **No alcohol, pork, or gambling imagery** — same reason.
- 🚫 No generic wallet, no generic coin stack, no generic upward arrow, no dollar sign. These are
  the four clichés of the category and they say nothing.
- 🚫 Nothing that reads as a bank or a card issuer — Buddget is not a bank and should not borrow
  their visual authority.
- 🚫 No emoji, no mascot with a face, no 3D bevels, no drop shadows inside the mark.

---

## What would make it right

- **Legible at 16px.** Test it there before anywhere else. If the idea needs detail to read, it is
  the wrong idea. Most of its life is a 26px header glyph and a home-screen icon seen at a glance.
- **Works as a silhouette** — one flat colour, no gradient, no outline.
- **Squares well.** iOS uses a squircle and Android an adaptive mask that can crop to a circle, so
  the mark needs a safe zone and must not rely on corners.
- **Neutral to script.** It sits next to Arabic and Latin text; it should not look like a Latin
  letterform.
- Ideas genuinely worth exploring: the *buddy* in the name (companionship, something alongside you
  rather than above you); money **in and out** as a single continuous form; a container that fills;
  the idea of *seeing clearly* — the tagline is "finally makes sense".

---

## 5. Deliverables for the chosen direction

1. **The mark** as clean SVG — flat single-colour version and gradient version, light and dark.
2. **Size proofs**: 16px, 26px (in-app header), 64px, 512px. Show the 16px one honestly, not scaled
   down from large.
3. **App icons**: iOS squircle (1024×1024, no transparency, no pre-applied corner radius) and
   Android adaptive (foreground + background layers, safe zone respected, cropped to a circle as a
   proof).
4. **The wordmark lockup**: mark + "Buddget" horizontal, with clear-space and minimum-width rules,
   light and dark.
5. **A monochrome version** — pure black on white and pure white on black, for cases where colour
   is unavailable.
6. **On-surface proofs**: the mark on the app's light page background `#F4F7F6`, the dark background
   `#0A100F`, and on a teal fill.
7. A short rationale — what the mark means and why it suits a calm, automation-first money app.

---

## Do not

- Skip straight to one option.
- Deliver only large sizes — the small sizes are where marks fail.
- Add a tagline into the lockup.
- Use any colour outside the design system.
- Produce a mark that only works in the gradient.
