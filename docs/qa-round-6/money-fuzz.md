# QA Round 6: money-fuzz (property-based stress of the pure money and date engines)

Covered: cycle math (cycleKeyFor / budgetMonthKeyFor / cycleKeyOf / getMonthRange / cycleRange / closedCycleKeys / pendingCarryKeys) in Cairo, New York, Riyadh; payday schedule engine (paydayDatesForWindow, shiftToBusinessDay) both weekend rules; amount parser and 16 formatters. Also covered: store action sequences (15 action types incl. savings deposit/withdraw/transfer/correct/delete, expenses, income, debt payments, savings-funded payoff) with net-worth conservation and apply-then-undo identity, FX conversion, installment rounding, 40k-row differential of computeMonthlyStats. NOT covered: BNPL schedule generation, ATM/top-up/FX-exchange SMS-created rows, card outstanding (`computeCreditCardBalance`), pocket-adjust and tracked-account balances (`computeBalances`), goal allocation and month-carry end-to-end, budget engine splits (only existing unit tests read), 2-currency gold in the store fuzz. Repros are in docs/qa-round-6/repro/money-fuzz/*.test.ts.txt (copy to src/__qa6__/money-fuzz/ without the .txt to run; prng.ts.txt is the shared generator).

Seeded PRNG: `mulberry32` (src/__qa6__/money-fuzz/prng.ts, moved to docs/qa-round-6/repro/money-fuzz/). Seeds are in each test file.
