## Copy style (from the owner's edits)

- **Open with the user's question, not the product.** "Where did your money go this month?" leads the promotional text and the description (EN and AR). The product answer follows in the next sentence.
- **One concrete promise, said plainly:** know what you can spend today ("Free to spend"), with rent, bills and goals already set aside.
- **Honest qualifiers stay:** "roughly what's free to spend", "with nothing to log by hand". No hype words, no superlatives, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- **Privacy is a short, calm, late line, never the pitch:** "We don't sell your data, and buddget shows no ads." The defensive "your bank login stays with your bank" line was removed from the U.S. text.
- **Structure:** a 2-sentence opening, then UPPERCASE section heads with 3 or 4 "•" bullets each. Bullets are one plain sentence, feature then benefit. Then Free, Pro, the 30-day Pro trial, privacy, and subscription terms last.
- **Words:** "buddget" is always lowercase; "Buddgy" is the assistant; "Free to spend", "Flow", "Accounts", "Pockets", "Paydays", "Sparks", "the Advisor". Use "installments" (U.S.), "bank texts" on iOS (the Shortcut) and "bank SMS" on Android.
- **Arabic:** clear Modern Standard Arabic, with a light Egyptian touch only in the hook ("كم تقدر تصرف اليوم"). Use the in-app terms exactly: المتاح للصرف، بادجي، بادجت برو، شرارات (Sparks), المستشار (the Advisor).

**Description (3392/4000)**

```text
Where did your money go this month? Know what you can spend today, with rent, bills and goals already set aside.

buddget shows one figure you can act on: Free to spend. It's worked out from your accounts, cards, installments, debts and goals, and it moves with every expense. Set money aside inside the account where it actually sits. It works in more than ten currencies, from Egyptian pounds to riyals and dirhams, and counts gold too.

YOUR MONTH AT A GLANCE

• Flow shows what's left to spend today and each expense as it happens
• Accounts groups your banks, wallets, cards and cash, with net worth beside Free to spend
• Money set aside for a goal stays in its bank account, so your total never looks bigger than it is

INSTALLMENTS AND DEBTS, ON SCHEDULE

• Installment and buy-now-pay-later plans at the full price you agreed, in fixed payments, with every due date and what's left to pay
• Each payment is the agreed price divided by the number of payments. No interest is calculated.
• A plan bought on a credit card shows in that card's balance, where you will actually pay it
• Money you owe and money owed to you, with a reminder on the pay-back date

SAVING THAT ADDS UP

• Pockets for anything you're setting aside, and goals with a target and a date
• One goal can sit in several places: part in your bank, part in dollars, part in gold
• At month end, buddget shows your leftover and where it would go. One tap sets it aside.

INCOME AS IT REALLY ARRIVES

• Paydays for your salary, with the next date always in view
• Freelance, rent or family money counts the day it arrives
• Link each payment to its payday to see what has arrived and what is still to come

LOGGING IN SECONDS

• Type it, say it, or snap the receipt
• Turn on the optional Shortcut and your bank's texts reach buddget, so card payments log themselves
• Every screen in Arabic, right to left, or in English

BUDDGY, YOUR MONEY ASSISTANT

Ask Buddgy about your month and get an answer built from your own accounts, spending and plans. Buddgy can also help you build a budget from your income.

A WEEKLY HABIT

Keep your weekly streak, earn points and move up tiers as you track.

FREE

Every account, bank-text tracking, budgets, goals, debts, installments, reports and export. Buddgy, voice entry and receipt scans come with a set allowance.

BUDDGET PRO

About ten times the AI allowance, the Advisor (Buddgy's reasoning model for big money decisions), unlimited saved budget plans and unlimited live balance checks. Sparks top up your AI use whenever you need more.

YOUR FIRST 30 DAYS ARE PRO

Every new account gets every Pro feature for 30 days, with no card. Afterwards you move to Free automatically. Nothing is charged and nothing is deleted.

PRIVACY

We don't sell your data, and buddget shows no ads.

SUBSCRIPTION TERMS

buddget Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price shown in the app. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. Sparks you buy are one-time purchases and never expire.
```

## Issues found in App Store Connect (not changed, waiting for the owner)

1. **Arabic keywords:** `تتبع،استثمار،اموال` uses Arabic commas. Apple splits only on `,`, so it counts as one keyword. Fix: `تتبع,استثمار,أموال`.
2. **English (U.K.) is still the older text:** its promotional text and description have no opening question and no PRIVACY section, and still carry the "bank login" line. The U.K. page is primary in Egypt and the Gulf, so it should match the U.S. text (with U.K. spelling if wanted).
3. **U.S. keywords repeat name words:** "budget" and "expense" are already indexed from the name, which wastes 15 characters. Suggested swaps: `sms,bnpl,salary`.
4. **Arabic terms differ from the app:** the store says "سبارك" and "المساعد", while the app says "شرارات" and "المستشار". Fix the AR description and the 5 Sparks names (٦٠ شرارة …). Also "اضعاف" should be "أضعاف" in the Pro descriptions.
5. **App Accessibility not started:** the declared features show on the product page. The owner must declare only what the app really supports. Likely yes: Dark Interface, Larger Text (check 200%), Sufficient Contrast. Verify VoiceOver before claiming it.
6. **Optional, not blocking:**
   - a 1024×1024 image per in-app purchase or subscription (used for offer codes and win-back);
   - no English (U.K.) localization on the products (U.K. users see the U.S. names);
   - In-App Events and Custom Product Pages are empty;
   - iPhone Duo screenshot slots are new and empty.
