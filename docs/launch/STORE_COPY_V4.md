## Positioning

Value propositions, in the order the copy leads with them:
1. **One clear daily number.** Free to spend across every account and currency, after money set aside for rent, bills and goals.
2. **Installments and debts on schedule.** Every plan at the full agreed price, with dates and what's left, plus money owed both ways with reminders.
3. **It logs itself, and it answers back.** Bank texts become entries (SMS on Android, an optional Shortcut on iPhone), and Buddgy answers from your own numbers, in Arabic or English.

Privacy appears once per description, as one plain sentence after the intro: "It runs on what you add and the bank texts you choose to share. Your bank login stays with your bank." No privacy section, no privacy frame.

App Store copy names only the iPhone Shortcut and the Apple Account; Play copy names only bank SMS and the Google Play account (App Review guideline 2.3.10 rejects metadata that mentions other platforms).
