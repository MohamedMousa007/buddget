## 2. Ranking and conversion

**App Store** ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)):
- Limits: name 30, subtitle 30, keywords 100, separated by commas with no spaces.
- Apple says: no plurals, category names, "app", duplicates or competitor or trademark names. Repeating a name or subtitle word in the keywords wastes space (common ASO practice, not Apple's wording).
- Promotional text (170) does not affect ranking and can change at any time.
- Only the description's first sentence shows before "more".
- The first 1 to 3 screenshots appear in search results. Include one Dark Mode screenshot.
- Previews: up to 3, each 30 seconds or less, autoplaying muted.
- Rating prompts: at most 3 a year per user ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/requesting-app-store-reviews)).
- In-app events: up to 10 live, names of 30 characters or less, 31 days or less each ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/offer-in-app-events/offer-in-app-events)).

**Localization (key finding).** In Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the other Gulf storefronts the default language is **English (U.K.)** and Arabic is the extra one ([Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-store-localizations)).
Fill both: two keyword fields (200 characters) per market. Whether US-English-only listings are indexed there is unverified. English keywords stay popular there and Arabic terms surface different apps ([AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/why-you-should-localize-your-aso-in-hebrew-arabic)).

**Google Play** ([policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842)):
- Limits: title 30, short description 80, full description 4,000 (from Play Console). The full description is indexed, so keywords belong there naturally.
- Banned: "#1" and "best", price or promotion in the title, unattributed testimonials, keyword repetition.
- Never trigger a review prompt from a button. The quota is not published ([Android](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review)).
- Promotional content eligibility: not verified.

**Evidence is thin.** "70% never scroll" figures are unsourced; no video data ([AppFollow](https://www.appfollow.io/blog/app-screenshots-guide-for-app-store-optimization/)). One real test: reordering screenshots moved installs 6.4% ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/case-study-how-a-slash-b-testing-can-improve-your-apps-conversion-rates)). Run Product Page Optimization and Play listing experiments.

**Rating prompts:** ask after a month-end leftover is saved, a goal is reached or a streak is hit. Never after an SMS error.

## 3. Keywords

Evidence: Play searches (EG, SA, AE) and Google Trends Egypt (web, not store, searches).

- **مصاريف / مصروفات:** the Egypt Play results are about 30 small Arabic trackers led by Al-Masareef. On Trends its summer peak is school fees. Lead Arabic term.
- **ميزانية:** in the Saudi Play results next to Wallet. On the web it means the state budget. Secondary.
- **تقسيط / أقساط:** steady on Trends (9 against 1 for ميزانية), but the Play results are lenders (Valu, Halan, بسيطة, Contact, Souhoola). The volume is high and the intent is a lender, so use it as a differentiator: tracking the plans you already have.
- **ادخار، ديون، راتب، ذهب، جنيه/ريال/درهم:** no evidence found. Arabic keyword field.
- **English:** "budget", "expense tracker", "spending". UAE Play results for "sms expense tracker" are about 6 small apps, none dominant.

Name Valu, Sympl and Contact only in the description, never in keywords. Test after launch: whether Apple treats a word with and without "ال", and singular and plural, as the same keyword.

## 4. Positioning

**Lead with these three:**
1. **Installments and BNPL inside your budget**, including Sharia-compliant full-price tracking. No rival leads with it, and lenders hold the Arabic term.
2. **Automatic, with no bank link.** Bank SMS on Android, an optional Shortcut on iPhone. Rivals either link banks (Wallet and Drahim reviews report sync failures) or are tiny.
3. **Arabic everywhere, in EGP, the Gulf currencies and gold.** Of the global apps, only Wallet has Arabic.

Support: real-balance accuracy, month-end leftover to savings, Buddgy from your numbers.

**Privacy.**
- **Rivals:** Pockito: short "No bank login", yet declares tracking. Monee: no data collected. Drahim: central-bank licence. Western leaders: barely mention it.
- **V3 opens with five privacy bullets before any benefit, which reads defensive.** Put the benefit first.
- **State it once, early, as a plain fact:** "No bank login. You choose which texts it reads." Then keep three bullets: off until you turn it on; Android sees new texts only and iPhone only what your Shortcut sends; no ads, never sold, delete anytime.
- **Give it one screenshot.** Avoid "secure" and "bank-grade".
- **Keep `PRIVACY_DECLARATIONS.md` consistent with the copy.**
