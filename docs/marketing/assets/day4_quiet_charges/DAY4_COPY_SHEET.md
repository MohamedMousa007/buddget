Reels and TikToks are the formats that can reach people who don't follow you; Stories and polls mostly reach existing followers, so they wait until there is an audience. Nothing here guarantees reach. Reply to every comment in the first day or two, keep posting the same question at the end of each video, and judge results against your own earlier posts.
