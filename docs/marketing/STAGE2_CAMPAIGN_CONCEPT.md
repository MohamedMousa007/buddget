## Campaign idea

**"What's actually left?"** (Egyptian Arabic: «فاضل فعلًا كام؟»)

Insight (OWNER BELIEF, to validate): people check the bank balance, but the balance is the number before instalments, subscriptions, rent and goals. The real question is what is actually left.

Three acts: (1) **The question**, problem awareness, no product; (2) **One picture**, brand, the whole month in one picture; (3) **Show it**, reveal onwards, real screens from a verified build.

Challenge: "what's left" is close to the safe-to-spend wording competitors already use. We own the question, tone and wider picture, not the calculation, and make no comparative claim. The "left" figure in the tester build must be verified before the reveal.

## Tagline options

1. Know what's actually left. «اعرف فاضلك فعلًا كام» (recommended: awareness signature)
2. Your whole month, one picture. «شهرك كله في صورة واحدة» (brand descriptor from the reveal)
3. Month-end, minus the surprise. «آخر الشهر من غير مفاجآت»
4. Money, made clear. «فلوسك واضحة»
5. Your money, finally makes sense. «فلوسك أخيرًا مفهومة» (existing product line; "finally" needs a no-shame check)

Taglines sit beside the logo, never inside the lockup.

## Visual system (existing assets only)

Teal `#0D8578` fill, `#12B0A0` bright, soft `#E3F6F3`, ink `#0A100F`, mist `#F4F7F6`, alert red `#BE343A` for warnings only (not in awareness posts). DM Sans, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, JetBrains Mono (Latin digits, as in the app). Logo from `design/buddget-logo`; Buddgy from `src/components/ai/BuddgyIcon.tsx`. Five devices: mist paper, ledger leaders, the marker, one big question, mono numbers. Avoid coins, rising arrows, stock people, neon gradients, clichés, fear red.

## Buddgy

Signature and gentle question-asker, not the host. Recognition: visor face in teal, the single question, a fixed sign-off spot. About one post in three early. Moods: curious, thinking, done, wink, listening, sleepy. No alert, worried or error faces in awareness content. Never mocks spending, never advises. Existing artwork only; motion built from the existing mood animations by a motion editor.

## How the name arrives (by campaign week, not by launch date)

Weeks 1 to 2: wordmark as sign-off with the question. Weeks 3 to 4: Buddget as calm narrator of short money notes, first Buddgy cameo. Weeks 5 to 6: Meet Buddgy. Weeks 7 to 8: "we're building Buddget", behind the scenes, beta list (exists on the site). Reveal: gated on launch readiness in a verified build; no date, price or bank names.

## Localisation (drafts, unreviewed)

Egypt «فاضل كام؟ / فاضل فعلًا كام؟»; Saudi «وش باقي معك فعلًا؟»; UAE «كم باقي لك فعلًا؟ / شو اللي باقي؟». Saudi and UAE are a thin layer, manual and voice only, no SMS claims. A native reviewer per market before publishing.

## Seasonal

Ramadan and Eid modules plug in where natural (household and food, giving, Eid, after the season). Guardrails: no fear, no guilt, no religious guidance, no prescribed amounts. Not the brand identity; no date tied to it. Timing NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## Production without a presenter

Carousels and Stories: owner and AI from templates, native Arabic review. Type and leader reels: AI-assisted drafts, editor polish. Screen recordings: owner records from a verified build, editor frames. Voice-over: human artist per dialect recommended (AI dialect quality unverified; scratch only). Buddgy motion: motion designer.
