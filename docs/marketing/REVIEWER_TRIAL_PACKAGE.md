## Trial (about 2 hours, fixed fee, 48 h, one revision round)
Rules: no shame; no advice, promises or fear; "we" voice; "Buddget" and "Buddgy" stay Latin; no literal translation.
1. Localize six English lines (top choice + alternative + reason): "What's actually left?" · "The salary lands in one place. A few days later, where is it?" · "It's not that we're not paying attention. The picture itself isn't in one place." · "A subscription? When did we sign up for that?" · "The one we meant to cancel… renewed." · "Save this if your money is spread across more than one place."
2. Review three drafts (Post 2 caption, Post 3 voice-over, Post 5 on-screen lines): OK / Edit / Replace + reason.
3. Judgment check (Use / Fix / Reject + why): «خمس أماكن لمرتب واحد. حتى أنا هحتاج دقيقة.» · «صرفت كتير تاني!» · «اشترِ دهب دلوقتي.» · «زكاتك لسه ما اتحسبتش؟» · «بيجوا في هدوء.» (b, c, d are deliberate tests; do not say so.)
4. Terminology: bank balance, what's left, payday, salary, instalment, subscription, rent, household money, mobile wallet, cash, card, budget.
5. Note: up to 3 unnatural or translated-sounding items, 1 too formal, any cultural risk.

## Scoring (pass at 85/100; points = weight × score/5, score 0 to 5)
Natural Egyptian Arabic 25% · Cultural relevance 20% · Marketing copywriting quality 20% · Brand voice consistency 15% · Financial terminology accuracy 10% · Creativity and humour 10%.
Automatic rejection: a serious mistranslation that changes the intended meaning; culturally inappropriate content introduced or passed through without a flag (including missing the shaming, advice and religious-pressure lines in Task 3).
Record: reviewer and date; comment and correction per criterion; key corrections; final pass/fail (Pass valid only if the computed result is PASS).
