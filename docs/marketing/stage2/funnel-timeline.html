The funnel, post by post.

Your five funnel stages mapped onto the approved campaign, with a week-by-week plan of posts for Instagram and TikTok. I used the stages and the order from your image, not its 3D rainbow look, because Buddget's brand system is teal-led and flat. No date is announced: the timeline runs in weeks from Day 1 and from the launch date, which is not set.

1 · The funnel mapped to the campaign

How the stages connect to the three acts

  • Awareness = Act 1, The Question. Be recognised. No product.
  • Consideration = Act 2, The Picture. The idea, Buddgy, and what Buddget is. The profile becomes the information hub.
  • Engagement = Act 3 begins: real screens, real answers, trust, and a conversation. Gated on verified capability.
  • Conversion = pre-launch and launch: the full tour, honest FAQ, store links.
  • Retention = after launch: activation, education, community, and a new question every month.

The default timeline puts the launch date L at the start of week 15 (Day 1 is about 14 weeks before L). That is a planning anchor, not a date.

What if the dates differ? The date is not set; readiness decides it, not the calendar.
  • If the launch is sooner (the earliest window I estimated is late January to mid-February 2027): compress. Minimums are Awareness 3 weeks, Consideration 3, Engagement 3, Conversion 4. That puts Day 1 about 10 weeks before L.
  • If the gates slip: don't announce a date. Extend Engagement with the holding pattern: money notes, "Buddgy asks", Q&A, behind the scenes and "what we changed". These need no verified feature.
  • If a capability isn't verified by its week, swap its post for the next verified one or a holding-pattern post. Never show an unverified feature.

The month-end question cycle

Each month the campaign asks a new question and answers it on a real screen. Month 1 keeps «فاضل فعلًا كام؟». Later questions are drafted from what the community actually asks. That keeps the story going after launch.

What retention content never does

  • No fake testimonials, ratings or statistics.
  • No streak guilt or "you haven't opened the app" pressure.
  • No promise of financial outcomes.
  • No mention of the paid plan, prices or the founders club until they are built and approved.
4 · The rules that hold across every stage
RuleHow it shows up in the plan
No shame, no fearEvery hook is about a situation, never a flaw; "we" voice throughout
No unverified claimEvery product row carries a gate; nothing publishes until the claims checklist is green
CTA ladderSave, share, vote → follow, answer → beta list (W7, gated) → try or get the app (W13 onward) → use a feature
BuddgyUnnamed and small in Act 1; named in W5; asks, never advises; about one post in three early; a pointer only in demos
Native reviewEach batch goes to the Egyptian reviewer before approval; Saudi and UAE reviewers join when those markets start
EvidencePoll results, quotes and numbers are shown only if real, with counts and permission
ProductionOne batch of five ideas a week; never more than one unreviewed batch in the pipeline
How to use this. The first batch is already written. When it's reviewed and published, Batch 2 (week 2) is written next from the rows above, and each later week follows the same cycle: write, native review, approve, produce, publish, measure, adjust.
