# Meet Buddgy

Buddgy is the assistant inside Buddget. Think of a friend who is good with money: it knows your numbers, answers plainly, and can do the tapping for you. It never changes anything without showing you first.

## Opening Buddgy

Tap the round Buddgy face on the edge of the screen. You can drag it to either side. If you are signed out, Buddgy asks you to sign in first, because it needs your data to be useful.

## What you can ask

**Understand your money**
- "How much did I spend this month?" / "كم صرفت هذا الشهر؟"
- "What's left to spend?"
- "Where is my money going?" Buddgy checks your spending by category, payment method, month or merchant.
- "What's my net worth?"
- "Who do I still owe?" and "Which subscriptions am I paying for?"

**Get advice**
- "How can I cut my food spending?"
- "Should I pay off my card or save first?"
- "Help me plan for a trip in March."

Advice comes as short steps with the key numbers in bold. Buddgy is a helper, not a financial advisor. For tax, legal or investment-product decisions, check with a professional.

**Get things done**
- Log: "coffee 45", "قهوة ٤٥", "paid 300 to Ahmed", "salary 15k today".
- Change: "make that 60", "move it to Groceries".
- Remove: "delete the taxi expense".
- Manage: debts, installments, credit cards, subscriptions, savings pockets, goals, budgets, payment methods, income and paydays.
- Settings: "switch to dark mode", "show a second currency", "my month starts on the 25th".
- Go somewhere: "open my goals", "add a payment method".

Buddgy answers in the language you write in, English or Arabic.

## How changes work

1. You ask.
2. Buddgy shows a card with exactly what it will do: the amount, the category, the date.
3. You tap the button to confirm, or **Edit** to adjust a field, or **Not now**.
4. Once it is done you get an **Undo** button.

Two things to know:
- **Small, clear entries** such as "coffee 45" are added right away. You see "Done" and an **Undo**, so nothing happens behind your back.
- **Deletes and bigger changes** always wait for your tap, and the button is red for deletes.

Buddgy will not touch your password, two-step sign-in, bank-message tracking permissions, backups, or your account itself. Those stay in Settings.

## Quick answers that work offline

Short notes like "coffee 45" and simple questions like "what's left to spend?" are understood on your phone. They are instant and work without internet. Anything that needs real thinking needs a connection. If you are offline, Buddgy tells you so.

## Remembering you

If you tell Buddgy something lasting ("I'm saving for a wedding in June", "my rent is paid on the 1st"), it can offer to remember it and shows you a card to accept. You can see and remove everything it remembers under **Settings → Buddgy**. Turn learning off there if you prefer.

## Starting over

- The pencil icon at the top starts a **new chat**.
- A chat you leave for about half an hour starts fresh on its own.
- Chats are not stored on our servers. Only the short list of things Buddgy remembers is kept.

## If Buddgy says it is resting

Buddgy has fair-use limits so it stays quick for everyone. If you reach one, it tells you when it will be back. Your data and the quick offline entries are never affected.

## Tips for good answers

- Be specific about amounts and dates when you have them. "Rent 12000 on the 1st" beats "add rent".
- Say which account or card if it is not your usual one: "with CIB card".
- You can send several things in one message: "coffee 45, lunch 120 and 300 to Ahmed". Each one gets its own card.
- If Buddgy asks a question, it is because a wrong guess would cost you. Pick one of the options.

## Privacy in one minute

- Buddgy only reads your data to answer you, and only the parts it needs.
- What you type, and the facts it needs to answer, are sent securely to our AI provider to produce the reply. We do not store your conversations.
- Everything Buddgy changes is applied by the app on your device, after you approve it.
