Settings redesign · for review

Pro row, Settings order and profile menu

Mockups for the three layout points you raised, after a design critique and a four-voice council (Skeptic, Pragmatist, Critic, Founder). Each section shows today next to the proposal, and says whether it needs your decision. Phone frames are a 375 pt screen drawn at 300 px.

1 · Pro entry

Replace the aurora card with one membership row

Three problems came up. The Sparkles icon means AI everywhere else in the app. The teal aurora is the same gradient as the page heroes, so Pro looks like another hero, not a membership. And a 76 px card at the top makes Settings open like an ad. The proposal is one row with a dark seal edged in gold, the only gold on the page, so it still reads as premium. The words on the right change with your plan.

Council: all four voices agree. Remove the card, keep Sparkles for AI only, and make Pro a row whose status changes with the plan. A tab was ruled out: Pro is a state of your account, not a place you visit.

2 · Settings page

A grouped list in the order people expect

The order follows iOS Settings, WhatsApp and Revolut: you, then your plan, then how the app behaves, then privacy and data, then help. Today the page mixes five kinds of block (banner, gradient card, call-to-action card, tile row, lists). That mix is what makes it feel crowded, more than the number of rows. The proposal uses one kind: rows in titled groups. The quick tiles become ordinary rows under Preferences, and the invite card becomes a row.

Council: all four voices gave the same grouping. Usage moves inside the Pro page. Founders Club appears only for founders. Sign out moves to the bottom of Settings.

3 · Profile menu

A short menu, not a second Settings page

Today the menu is about 15 rows tall and repeats Settings: the quick tiles, Help and the Pro row are in both places. Goals, Paydays, Subscriptions, Payment methods and Reports have no other way in, so they can't simply be dropped.

Council: split on the shape. All four agree to remove the duplicates (tiles, Help). The Critic ruled out a side drawer: it fights the Android back gesture and the edge swipe, and Arabic keeps a left-to-right layout. The Pragmatist and Critic want a short dropdown. The Skeptic wants the avatar to open a full page. The Founder wants a sheet.
