What changes and why

Two pages, clear jobs

  • Plan and billing: your member card, managing the subscription, billing history.
  • Usage: the meters, the other limits, and Sparks. You buy Sparks where you run out.
  • Sparks receipts also show in billing history, so every payment is in one list.

The member card

  • Obsidian in dark, pearl in light. One hairline, fine grain, a faint teal bloom and a large blind-embossed "b". No gold, no avatar.
  • Front: your name, plan and billing period, member since, renewal date and member number.
  • Back: your Pro benefits and the billing line: store, price and next charge.
  • Tap to flip: it lifts toward you mid-turn, a light sweeps across and its shadow pulls in.

Free gets a showcase, not a locked card

  • The top of the page compares what you have with what Pro adds, side by side, with Pro raised and outlined.
  • One primary action, "Start 30 days free", with "No card" under it. Yearly is preselected with the monthly equivalent shown.

Rewards

  • Five tiers: Silver, Emerald, Diamond, Elite (Pro only) and Elite Founder (a founder who reaches Elite).
  • The tier card is letterpress: thick soft-touch stock in the tier colour, a blind-embossed b and foil-stamped type that catches the light. Tap it to see the tier's perks.
  • Your 12-week hot streak with freezes, the tier ladder, ways to earn, and your badge on your profile and in Settings.

Founders club

  • A black card with a gold hairline, your founder number and your name as a signature, with a line of thanks.
  • At most 100 founders; the window closes 60 days after launch.

Rules kept

  • Only the app's own tokens: brand teal, green and amber for states, DM Sans and JetBrains Mono.
  • Absolute dates, no countdowns or fake scarcity. Cancelling and payment changes go through the App Store.

Sparks pricing and rewards economy: final

How the app charges: 1 Spark per $0.001 of real model cost, rounded up on every call, so a Spark never costs us more than $0.001 (migration 0298). Prices include VAT (Egypt 14%, Saudi 15%, UAE 5%); the store keeps 15%; 54.5 EGP to the dollar. A Spark is counted at its full $0.001 even though rounding up makes most cost less, so every margin below is a floor.

Correction. An earlier version of this page said packs lose money. That was wrong: it priced Sparks per feature (an old table the app no longer uses) at the wrong exchange rate. Streak gifts

  • 4 weeks 10 Sparks · 12 weeks 30 · 26 weeks 60 · 52 weeks 120.
  • Gifts expire after 60 days with a countdown chip; they are always spent before bought Sparks, which never expire.

Keeping members

  • Win-back: 30% off Pro monthly for 2 months, once per account.
  • Save offer: 10% off the next year, once, for members of 12 months or more who turn auto-renew off.
  • Founders: 20% off their first renewal (12 months); never a forever price.

Show the value

  • Pack sheet: "650 Sparks + 65 Diamond bonus (worth EGP 10)".
  • Monthly recap: "Diamond gave you EGP 48 this year", shareable as a letterpress card.
  • Tier-up moment: full-screen card, new app icon, the gift and its countdown. Monday: "Your Diamond credits are in".

What it costs (worst / expected per year)

  • A steady Free member reaching Diamond: $0.57 / $0.23. Covered if about 5 in 100 such members go Pro who would not have.
  • An Elite Pro member: $0.46 (10% of what they earn us) / $0.18 (4%).
  • Member price on Pro is an experiment: half of eligible members see it; it switches off unless at least 1.4 times as many buy (the break-even).
  • Win-back at 30% off still earns $0.49 a month after AI.

Council

Skeptic

Stacking bonuses broke the 33% floor by design, and heavy users who reach Elite are the costly ones. Keep pack bonuses to one, don't add weekly credits for Pro, and fix the yearly plan's heavy-user loss first.

Pragmatist

Bonuses must never stack; show value in EGP, not Sparks; make tier-ups a moment with a countdown on the gift; spend cash perks only where someone is about to pay or leave.

Critic

Saudi, not Egypt, was the tightest market on the old packs. Refunds after use need clawback of the bonus. Gifts need one account per device. Store 15% rates need enrolment. Tier offers need offer codes, not introductory offers.

Verdict

All adopted: one bonus at a time, Pro +15%, no weekly credits for Pro, a once-per-account win-back and save offer, the Pro member price run as a measured experiment, and refund clawback on bonuses.

Still for you to decide

  • Heavy Pro users on the yearly plan in Egypt. A member who uses the whole weekly limit every week costs about $3.67 a year more than EGP 499 brings in (typical use leaves $4.73). Options: watch for 8 weeks of real data, or lower Egypt's weekly limit for yearly members to 120 credits.
  • Family Sharing on Pro shares it with up to 6 people. Recommended off at launch.
  • Store enrolment: confirm Apple Small Business and Google's 15% tier, which all of these numbers assume.
