## Narrative
Most brand/terms pages for Saudi telcos, delivery apps and retailers were unreachable (JS shells, 404s, Arabic PDFs). Per brief, unknown is not allowed, so every YELLOW without positive evidence is USE_INITIALS. No service had explicit permission for referential logo use (no GREEN).

- **apple_one** (RED): Apple guidelines for third parties: Apple logo and other Apple graphic symbols may not be used without a written licence; word-mark references allowed in limited 'compatible with' style; no altered/abbreviated marks; non-affiliation disclaimers expected. Read 2026-10-18. Only Apple-owned graphic symbols exist for Apple One, so the logo is off-limits without licence. Plain text name 'Apple One' is fine. URLs: https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html
- **stc_tv** (YELLOW): stc terms page title found but body not readable; no brand centre found. stc asserts registered ownership of its group logo (Argaam/Arab News reports).
