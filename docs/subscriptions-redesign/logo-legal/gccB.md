## Narrative
No service had positive evidence (explicit referential-use permission), so all 23 are USE_INITIALS. Counts: {'YELLOW': 19, 'RED': 4}.

### RED
- **Anytime Fitness**: Terms of Use: trademarks, service marks and trade dress on the site are owned by Anytime Fitness and others; nothing grants a licence to use any displayed trademark without prior written permission of the owner. (https://www.anytimefitness.com/terms-of-use/)
- **The National**: Terms: site material, including images, is protected by copyright/trademark and owned by The National FZ LLC; use other than personal, non-commercial use is a violation and prohibited. (https://www.thenationalnews.com/terms-and-conditions/)
- **e& (Etisalat) Mobile UAE**: Website T&Cs: site content (incl. images) is protected by copyright and trademark; may not be copied, reproduced, republished or transmitted without Etisalat's prior written permission; personal use only, no commercial use. No separate brand-centre page found (guessed URL 404). (https://www.eand.com/en/terms-and-conditions.html)
- **e& eLife home fibre**: Same e&/Etisalat website T&Cs: no reproduction of site content without prior written permission; personal use only. (https://www.eand.com/en/terms-and-conditions.html)

### YELLOW
Most are YELLOW because the policy page was unreachable, 404, or silent (unknown is not allowed). Tabby and Tamara publish brand kits but only for merchants, so they give no permission for a tracker. Government and transport marks (Absher, Sadad, Salik, Darb, Nol, Hafilat) are higher risk: affiliation could be implied and statutory protection is likely; Dubai's 2025 emblem law covers the Dubai emblem, not proven for these marks. Next steps: request written permission from Tabby, Tamara, e&, du, RTA if logos are wanted; read Tamara PDF and Tabby brand book manually.
