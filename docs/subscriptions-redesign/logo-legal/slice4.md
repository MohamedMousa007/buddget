## RED
- Apple Fitness+: Apple says only Apple/authorised resellers/licensees may use the Apple Logo in advertising; others need an express written licence to use it on websites/products. Word marks allowed in 'compatible with' phrasing, not prominent, no implied endorsement. Fitness+ is a listed Apple service mark. Page does not mention apps or Fitness+ logo specifically.
- Calm: Press page offers a press kit with logos for media (press@calm.com). Per a third-party summary of Calm's Terms (original page returned 403, not verified), trademarks/logos may not be used without prior written permission.
- Uber One: Uber's brand assets site says outside parties must get authorisation to display the Uber logo or reference Uber One. Uber general terms (s.3) bar use of Uber names/logos other than incidentally using the Services without written permission, including in app titles.

## YELLOW
Most YELLOW rows mean no official guideline page could be retrieved (unknown, not allowed). Strava, MyFitnessPal, Starlink, Orange and Vodafone have partial policy text in the JSON.
