First-run onboarding and Budget Studio, as built today

This page maps the onboarding journey and the budget question flow that ship in src/ today: every step, branch and question. It also shows what happened when the real graph and engine were run over 21,000 simulated people (3,000 for each of seven entry scenarios).

The short version

  • The design underneath is sound. A declarative graph of 106 questions with gates, follow-ups and whole-picture checks feeds a deterministic engine, and the AI only reads text and polishes the result.
  • One bug breaks most of it. When Buddgy reads the opener text and finds an answer from a later section, the question cursor jumps to that section and every earlier section counts as done. Core questions about income type, housing, household and debts are then never asked.
  • Even without that bug, the flow allows only four optional questions per run. Zakat is never asked in any scenario. With the mixed intent that onboarding uses, the question "Is there something big you are saving for?" is never asked either.
  • The progress percentage only counts questions still in the run, so it reaches 100% while the plan is still far from one built on full answers.
  • Onboarding asks payday, savings and intent, and the studio asks them again because it does not read them.

    G · Method and limits

    How these numbers were made

    • Real code, simulated people. The production graph (src/lib/budget/flow/*), the situation parser (situationAi.parseSituation) and engine v2 (engine.buildPlan) were bundled unchanged and driven the way StudioPage drives them: acceptDescribed for the opener, then nextQuestion, screenNodes and answer or skip per screen.
    • The population is synthetic. It is 50% Egypt, 25% UAE and 25% Saudi Arabia, with log-uniform income in local ranges. The answer mix is my estimate (for example 45% renters, 45% with debts, 55% with a goal). The rates scale with these assumptions; the structural results do not. The cursor jump, zakat never being asked and readiness reaching 100% happen in every population.
    • The opener is modelled, not called. Gemini was not called. Each fact the person holds that the AI can read was "said" with probability 45% (15% in the short-opener scenario). The parser then filtered it the way production does. The person skipped each skippable field with 15% probability.
    • What "applies" means. A question applies when its gate holds on the person's full true answers, settled over five passes.
    • What the simulation leaves out. Back and edit on the Known sheet, "Build now" (only the offer is counted) and the AI refinement step after the engine. It also never answers "not right" on a confirmation card, and it does not cover the manual, template or copy builders.
    • Engine issues from the v5 prototype work. A rent answered without housing is dropped, tracked utility bills are double-counted, a deficit removes everyday lines, and the reasons are in English only. These were not re-measured here.
    • Not checked. Nothing was run on a device. No real user data or analytics funnel was used. /api/analytics/onboarding has the real step drop-off and should be compared with this.
