BDG-020 Subscription: every screen in the flow

Phone-sized screens with the app's real copy. In order: the page for a Pro member, its end (where the new rows sit), the retention offer, the store hand-off, the states afterwards, the trial and payment-problem variants, Change plan, dark mode, and Billing help. The Manage bar, receipts, billing history and the three help rows are gone.

  • Removed from the page: Manage bar, Manage sheet, "How to cancel" row, "Problem with a purchase?" rows, Receipts sheet, Billing history list, and the standalone Restore button.
  • Kept on purpose: Restore purchases, inside Change plan and the free paywall only, because Apple requires a restore option for subscriptions. Android has no such rule, so it can go there later if you want.
  • Android: same screens; Apple wording becomes Google Play, and the retention offer shows only where the store has one.
