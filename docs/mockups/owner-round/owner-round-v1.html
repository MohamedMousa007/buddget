1 One height for every hero

Today the band grows when its note wraps or when it has no scale row, so the content under it jumps when you switch tabs (worst on narrow iPhones). Proposed: every band is 151px, the note is always one line, and a band with no scale row keeps that row's space.

Reuses HeroBand (contained) with the bar block always 38.75px and a one-line note slot; BandNoteLine drops whole parts from the end instead of wrapping.

2 One empty-state system

Today some screens use the shared empty state, others hand-roll a grey line or a bell, and the sizes differ. Proposed: every empty screen, section and picker uses EmptyState in its light-touch sizes, with one tone rule and one copy rule.

Reuses EmptyState variant="card" with size="page" (a screen) or size="section" (a block or a sheet), iconTone brand / positive / neutral, children for choice cards (SelectableCard as="action"), PendingRow. No new component.

5 Desktop: use the width

On a laptop, Flow, Accounts and Debts sit in one 640px column (PageGrid stack), leaving most of the screen empty. Proposed: the hero spans the content width (up to 1152px, the existing wide cap) and each page splits into a main column and a rail, like a dashboard. Phone and tablet layouts do not change.

Reuses Sidebar (176px, unchanged), PageContainer wide, PageGrid with its secondary rail, HeroBand, and the phone's existing cards and rows. These are skeletons: each block holds what the phone already shows.

? Open design questions

These need your call before the build starts. Everything else follows the plan and the council's card-flow decisions.

  1. Hero note overflow.When the note does not fit on one line, should the rest go behind a "+1" chip that shows the full note, or be dropped silently? And should the "exactly …" pill that appears on tap stay inside the fixed 151px as an overlay?
  2. First-account guide.Are three choice cards (Bank account, Card, Wallet or cash) right? Accounts found in bank messages would show only as a row once some exist, and the "Find my accounts" button goes.
  3. Virtual and store cards.The type bar keeps only Debit, Credit and Prepaid. Should Virtual and Store card move to the + menu's card list, or sit under Debit as a "Kind" row?
  4. Top-up choices.Four equal choices (My account, Cash, Somewhere else, It was income) as drawn, or three choices with an "It was income" switch under "Somewhere else", as the plan words it?
  5. The Accounts "Card" entry.Two text actions on the Banks header as drawn, or a dashed "Add a card" row under the bank list?
  6. Where the merge prompt appears.A dialog when Accounts opens, a PendingRow on Accounts that opens it, or both?
  7. Budget empty state.Keep Buddgy as the illustration as drawn, or use the plain tile like every other page?
  8. Plans strip.Should a tap on another plan card preview it on the page, or open the deck sheet straight away? And should "New plan" stay in the strip or only in the sheet?
  9. Desktop width and rails.Is the existing 1152px cap the limit, or should content go wider on large screens? Which blocks belong in each page's rail?
  10. Arabic layout.The app keeps a left-to-right layout for Arabic today. Keep that for this round, or plan full right-to-left mirroring as its own piece of work?
